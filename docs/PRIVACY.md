# Privacy policy

Home Assets is a private, self-hosted household inventory app used only by the members of one
household. It is not offered to the public.

- The app runs on a computer in the household and is reached over the home network.
- It reads and writes one Google Sheet that the household owns, using a service account the
  household created.
- With the household's explicit Google sign-in, it stores backup copies of its own database and
  photos in a folder of that Google Drive (scope `drive.file`: only files the app itself created).
- No data is sent to any third party, no analytics are collected, and no data is sold or shared.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the
  app can no longer access Drive.

Contact: the repository owner via GitHub.
